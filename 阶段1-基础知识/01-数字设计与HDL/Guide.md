**阶段 1，第 1 节** — 组合逻辑与时序逻辑、数字层面的存储技术，以及 **Verilog（及相关 HDL 实践）**，让你能描述日后将映射到 FPGA 与加速器的硬件。

本模块把原先分开的 **数字基础** 与 **HDL** 两条线合并为一个带编号的章节。按顺序完成下面两篇指南（若已掌握基本逻辑，也可并行）。

**Phase 1, section 1** — combinational and sequential logic, memory technologies at a digital level, and **Verilog (and related HDL practice)** so you can express hardware you will later map to FPGAs and accelerators.

This module merges what were previously separate **digital fundamentals** and **HDL** tracks into one numbered section. Work through the two guides below in order (or in parallel if you already know basic logic).

---

**1. Digital design fundamentals**

* **[Digital Design Fundamentals](01-数字设计基础/Guide.md)** — Number systems, Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential logic, memory (SRAM, DRAM, ROM), timing intuition, and project ideas (breadboard through small RTL blocks).

**2. Hardware description languages**

* **[Hardware Description Languages (HDLs)](02-硬件描述语言/Guide.md)** — Verilog syntax and semantics, modeling styles, testbenches, synthesis-oriented thinking, and small RTL projects.

---

**Next in Phase 1**

**[Computer Architecture and Hardware](../02-计算机体系结构与硬件/Guide.md)** — ISA through memory hierarchy and real platforms, so you know *what* your gates and RTL will sit next to in a system.
